About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Rhode Island College with a Ph.D. from the University of Rhode Island. My work sits at the intersection of algorithmic fairness, high-performance computing, and applied artificial intelligence.

My dissertation developed novel Markov Chain Monte Carlo algorithms for electoral redistricting — optimizing these methods for fairness, scalability, and interpretability. I have applied these frameworks to study racial gerrymandering in Alabama's congressional districts and demonstrated their effectiveness through large-scale parallel implementations.

Beyond redistricting, I build multimodal AI systems for medical diagnosis, generative AI solutions for higher education, and enjoy communicating mathematics through puzzles and games.

Scholarly Work

Research Projects

2025 – 2026

Partial Map MCMC for Electoral Redistricting

A novel MCMC framework that incrementally constructs districting plans while preserving legal and fairness constraints. Applied to Alabama's congressional districts with a 90%+ success rate in generating racially fair maps.

MCMC Redistricting Fairness ACM CS&Law
2024 – 2026

Parallel MCMC Algorithms for Large-Scale Redistricting

Designed and evaluated multiple parallel MCMC frameworks (EpMCMC-DM, EpMCMC-SM, MPpMCMC, MJpMCMC) to improve scalability and sampling efficiency. Introduced machine-independent evaluation methodologies based on growth scaling.

HPC Parallel Computing IPDPS
2024 – 2025

Multimodal AI for Medical Diagnosis

Transformer-based multimodal architectures combining radiology reports and chest X-ray images for classification of 14 pathologies. Investigated vision–language model integration to improve diagnostic accuracy and clinical interpretability.

Transformers Vision-Language FLAIRS
2023 – 2025

Generative AI Systems for Higher Education

Designed and deployed production AI chatbots using AWS Lex, Amazon Bedrock, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation to automate student support services at URI, including financial aid and graduate admissions assistance.

RAG AWS Bedrock LLM EdTech
